### Restaurant ratings analysis:

## Introduction:
This project analysed restaurant ratings and consumer preferences in Mexico using Power BI to identify rating trends, consumer demographics, and potential restaurant investment opportunities.

## Key Metrics:
-	Total Consumers: 138
-	Total Restaurants: 130
-	Average Rating: 1.20
-	Average Consumer Age: 27 years
-	Highest-Rated Cuisine: Japanese (1.34)
-	Most Preferred Cuisine: Mexican

 ## Skills/Concepts Demonstrated:
-	Data cleaning and transformation
-	Data modelling
-	DAX measures and calculations
-	Interactive dashboards, slicers, and KPIs
-	Data visualisation and business insight generation

 ## Analysis:
- The highest rated restaurants serve Japanese, International and Brewery cuisine.  They are mostly medium/high priced restaurants and also provide alcohol service.
- Average ratings differ by preferred cuisine, consumer preferences are linked to how people rate.
- 82% of respondents are students, mostly within ages 20-24, 90% are single so the data sample is biased
- Demand and supply gaps exist. For example, 70% of consumers prefer Mexican but only 28 restaurants serve it. Latin American and Hot Dogs have demand but no supply

## Conclusion:
The analysis highlighted differences in restaurant ratings and consumer preferences. Restaurant investors should consider popular cuisines, appropriate pricing, customer experience, and target-market needs. 

## Recommendations:
- Survey families, older diners, higher spenders and other cities before generalizing results.
- Investment in restaurants should be based on medium or high price level, provision of alcohol service, market such as Cuernacava
- Address demand and supply gaps by optimizing more supply of consumer preferences.
